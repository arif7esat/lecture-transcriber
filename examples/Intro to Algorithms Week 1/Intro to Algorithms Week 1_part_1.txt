Okay, let's get started. Today we are talking about how to measure the speed of an algorithm. When we say an algorithm is fast, we do not mean seconds on your laptop. We mean how the number of steps grows as the input grows. That is what big-O notation describes. For example, if you look at every element of a list once, that is O(n). If you compare every element with every other element, that is O(n squared). This will definitely be on the exam, so make sure you can explain the difference. Now, let's look at searching. Linear search checks the elements one by one. In the worst case it looks at all n elements, so it is O(n).
